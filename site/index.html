A motion studio for your own product

Your interface, in motion. Three ways at once.

Point Motioneer at a running site, pick an element straight off the page, and get three motions for it side by side. Keep the one that feels right, then cut what you kept into a film.

$npx motioneer localhost:3000 Read how it works

Node 20 or newer. Nothing leaves your machine except the prompt to the model you chose.

  • Only transform and opacity move
  • Reduced motion respected
  • Checked in a real browser
  • Rendered to MP4 on your machine
