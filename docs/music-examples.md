# A musical identity for each film

Use these with `reel` for a new film, or `rescore` with an existing projectId to preserve the visual edit. Rescore saves a separate editable take and returns MP4, WAV and storyboard. Keep the original BPM for musical accents on the existing cut. Reels can be 6–30 seconds; these examples retain 16 seconds.

## Linear

> Keep the visual edit. Use rescore with music glass. Precise, luminous minimal electronica. A short glass-note signature, restrained sub pulses and microscopic clicks. Let the product holds breathe; bring the motif back for the closing lockup. Keep the original BPM and resolve on the closing brand card.
